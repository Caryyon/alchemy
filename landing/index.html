How to Play

Master the art of alchemy in three simple steps

01 🃏

Build Your Hand

Draw ingredient cards and spells from the shared market. Every card you take shapes your strategy.

02 ⚗️

Brew Potions

Assign mana from your hand to active recipes. Complete recipes to earn victory points and trigger powerful effects.

03 🏆

Conquer the Table

Use class powers, spells, and cunning to outmaneuver opponents. First to complete 3 recipes wins.
